Privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Pomodoro Chat is a focus timer published by Ronron Studio. In short: the app collects no personal data, shows no ads, requires no account and does not even have permission to access the Internet. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Data we collect

None. Ronron Studio does not receive, store or share any data about you. The app does not request the Android "Internet" permission: it is technically unable to send anything to a server.

There are no ads, no analytics, no automatic crash reporting and no embedded third-party services.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following locally, in its private storage: your collections and their periods (names, durations, Activity/Rest type), your session history (start and end times, completed or interrupted), your settings (theme, language, focus mode) and your custom themes.

If you pick a personal image or sound for a theme, a copy of the selected file is placed in the app’s private storage. Only the file you choose through the system picker is read; the app has no access to the rest of your photos or files.

This data never leaves your device because of Pomodoro Chat.

3. Permissions

Notifications: show the running timer and tell you when a period ends, even with the screen off. You can decline; the timer still works while the app is open.

Foreground service: keeps Android from stopping the timer when the screen is off or you switch apps.

No other permission is requested (no location, contacts, microphone, camera or Internet).

4. Android backup

If you turned on device backup (Google One / Android backup), the system may include the app’s data in the encrypted backup of your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google according to your settings; Ronron Studio has no access to it. You can turn it off in Android settings.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete a collection, a history entry or a theme directly in the app. To erase everything: Android Settings > Apps > Pomodoro Chat > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app.

6. Children and teens

Pomodoro Chat is designed for teenagers and suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected, no information about a minor is ever sent to Ronron Studio or any third party, and the app contains neither ads nor in-app purchases.

7. Windows version

The desktop version works the same way: all data stays in the computer’s user folder, with no network traffic at all.

8. Changes

If the way the app works changes (for example an optional sync feature), this policy will be updated on this page, with a new date, before that version is released.
