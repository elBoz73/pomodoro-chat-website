Get started in 8 quick steps.

  1. 1/8

    Meet your collections

    A collection is a ready-made work session: "Math homework", "History revision", "Piano practice"… Each row shows the number of periods and the total duration.

    The chart button opens your history, "+" creates a new collection and the pencil lets you edit one.

  2. 2/8

    Build your own collection

    Give it a name, then add as many periods as you like. For each one: a name, a duration in minutes and a type — Activity (you work) or Rest (you breathe).

    Classic Pomodoro is 25 min of activity followed by 5 min of rest, but it is up to you: 15 min of reading, 40 min of exercises… Save and you are ready.

  3. 3/8

    Start with the paw

    Open a collection: its periods are listed and the first one is highlighted. Tap the big paw to start, and tap it again to pause.

    The left button restarts the period, the right one completes it and moves on. A little meow (or the sound of your choice) marks the start, every period change and the end — with fireworks on top.

  4. 4/8

    Turn on focus mode

    In Settings, focus mode hides the list while a period is running: only the cat, the time and the buttons remain. Fewer distractions, more focus.

    As soon as you pause, the list comes back.

  5. 5/8

    Lock your screen, it keeps going

    The timer keeps running when the screen is off or you switch apps: a discreet notification shows the time left. Allow notifications on first launch to be told when each period ends.

    If your phone aggressively kills background apps, turn off battery optimisation for Pomodoro Chat.

  6. 6/8

    Track your progress

    History shows your focus time per day, week or month, compared with the previous week. Filter by collection, or by completed / interrupted sessions.

    Recorded a session by mistake? The cross on the right deletes it.

  7. 7/8

    Pick (or make) your theme

    Two themes are built in: Day, the zen cat in sage and terracotta, and Night, the astronaut cat among the stars.

    With "+ New theme", design your own: light or dark base, accent colour, font, text size, sounds, and even your own images for the cat, the paw or the background.

  8. 8/8

    Switch language

    The app speaks French, English and Spanish. Pick yours in Settings > Language; it is remembered.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

What is the Pomodoro technique?+

A simple way to work better: you alternate short focus periods (often 25 minutes) with small breaks (5 minutes). Your brain stays fresh, you procrastinate less and you can actually see your work moving forward.

Pomodoro Chat lets you set every period freely to match your own pace.

Is the app free?+

Yes, the download and the essentials are free: timer, collections, focus mode, Day and Night themes, history.

Premium, a one-time purchase (no subscription), unlocks custom themes and your own images, animated GIFs and sounds. No ads either way.

Do I need an account or an Internet connection?+

No account. The timer, collections and history work offline, airplane mode included. Internet is only used for the Premium purchase and, if you turn them on, anonymous statistics.

Is my data sent anywhere?+

Your collections, history, themes and images stay on your phone. Only anonymous usage statistics may be sent, and only if you turn them on in Settings. Details are in the privacy policy.

The timer stops when I turn off the screen. What can I do?+

It should keep going thanks to an ongoing notification. Check that notifications are allowed for Pomodoro Chat and, on some phones (Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung…), turn off battery optimisation for the app.

Is it suitable for middle and high schoolers?+

That is exactly who it was made for: a simple interface, a funny mascot, no ads, and statistics off by default. Perfect for homework, revision or practising an instrument.

Can I use my own images and sounds?+

Yes: create a custom theme, then replace the resting cat, the active cat, the paw or the background with your own images. Animated GIFs are supported, and start, period-change and end sounds can be customised too. Custom themes are part of Premium.

Is there an iPhone version?+

Not yet: Pomodoro Chat is available on Android.

Privacy policy▾

Last updated: October 6, 2026 · Pomodoro Chat app (studio.ronron.pomodoro)

Pomodoro Chat is a focus timer for Android, published by Ronron Studio. Your collections, history, themes, images and sounds stay on your phone: Ronron Studio runs no server that would store them. Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) are off by default. They are only sent if you explicitly turn them on in Settings → Privacy.

1. Summary

No account required. No ads in the app.

The timer, collections and history run entirely on the device, offline included.

2. Data stored on your device

Your collections and their periods (names, durations, Activity/Rest type).

Your session history (start and end times, completed or interrupted).

Your settings: theme, language, focus mode, statistics consent.

Your custom themes, and a copy of the images (GIFs included) and sounds you pick for them. Only the file you choose through the system picker is read; the app has no access to the rest of your photos or files.

Premium status (confirmation that a valid purchase exists through Google Play).

All of this is kept in the app’s private storage and is never sent to Ronron Studio.

3. Android permissions

Notifications (optional) — show the running timer and tell you when a period ends, even with the screen off. Without them, the timer still works while the app is open.

Foreground service — keeps Android from stopping the timer when the screen is off or you switch apps.

Internet — only to send anonymous statistics if you turned them on, and for purchases through Google Play.

No other permission is requested (no location, contacts, microphone or camera).

4. Anonymous statistics (Firebase Analytics)

Off by default. Turned on only after your explicit consent in Settings → Privacy.

When on, Pomodoro Chat may send a few anonymous technical events through Google Firebase Analytics, for example: session started or completed (planned duration and period type), focus mode turned on, theme chosen (Day, Night or “custom”), free version limit reached, successful Premium purchase.

Never sent: the names you type (collections, periods, themes), your images, your sounds, the details of your history, or any advertising ID used to target ads.

Google may process technical data under its own privacy policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app.

5. Premium purchases

The one-time Premium purchase is handled by Google Play Billing.

Ronron Studio neither receives nor stores your payment details; Google may collect payment information under its own terms.

Ronron Studio only receives confirmation that you own a valid Premium entitlement on this Google account.

6. Android backup

If you turned on device backup (Google One / Android backup), the system may include the app’s data in the encrypted backup of your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google according to your settings; Ronron Studio has no access to it.

7. Teens and families

Pomodoro Chat is designed for teenagers aged 13 and over. It is not intended for children under 13.

Statistics stay off until they are turned on, and the app contains no ads and builds no advertising profile.

Parental supervision is recommended for the Premium in-app purchase.

8. Deleting your data and your rights

In the app: you can delete a collection, a history entry or a theme.

To erase everything: Android Settings > Apps > Pomodoro Chat > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Statistics: withdrawing your consent or uninstalling the app stops any new collection. Already aggregated statistics cannot identify you individually.

For any GDPR request: use the contact address on the Google Play listing.

9. Changes

If the way the app works changes, this policy will be updated on this page, with a new date, before that version is released.

Contact: use the developer email address shown on the Pomodoro Chat Google Play listing.
