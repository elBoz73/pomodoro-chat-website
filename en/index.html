User guide

Get started in 8 quick steps.

  1. 1/8

    Meet your collections

    A collection is a ready-made work session: "Math homework", "History revision", "Piano practice"… Each row shows the number of periods and the total duration.

    The chart button opens your history, "+" creates a new collection and the pencil lets you edit one.

  2. 2/8

    Build your own collection

    Give it a name, then add as many periods as you like. For each one: a name, a duration in minutes and a type — Activity (you work) or Rest (you breathe).

    Classic Pomodoro is 25 min of activity followed by 5 min of rest, but it is up to you: 15 min of reading, 40 min of exercises… Save and you are ready.

  3. 3/8

    Start with the paw

    Open a collection: its periods are listed and the first one is highlighted. Tap the big paw to start, and tap it again to pause.

    The left button restarts the period, the right one completes it and moves on. A little meow (or the sound of your choice) marks the start, every period change and the end — with fireworks on top.

  4. 4/8

    Turn on focus mode

    In Settings, focus mode hides the list while a period is running: only the cat, the time and the buttons remain. Fewer distractions, more focus.

    As soon as you pause, the list comes back.

  5. 5/8

    Lock your screen, it keeps going

    The timer keeps running when the screen is off or you switch apps: a discreet notification shows the time left. Allow notifications on first launch to be told when each period ends.

    If your phone aggressively kills background apps, turn off battery optimisation for Pomodoro Chat.

  6. 6/8

    Track your progress

    History shows your focus time per day, week or month, compared with the previous week. Filter by collection, or by completed / interrupted sessions.

    Recorded a session by mistake? The cross on the right deletes it.

  7. 7/8

    Pick (or make) your theme

    Two themes are built in: Day, the zen cat in sage and terracotta, and Night, the astronaut cat among the stars.

    With "+ New theme", design your own: light or dark base, accent colour, font, text size, sounds, and even your own images for the cat, the paw or the background.

  8. 8/8

    Switch language

    The app speaks French, English and Spanish. Pick yours in Settings > Language; it is remembered.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

What is the Pomodoro technique?+

A simple way to work better: you alternate short focus periods (often 25 minutes) with small breaks (5 minutes). Your brain stays fresh, you procrastinate less and you can actually see your work moving forward.

Pomodoro Chat lets you set every period freely to match your own pace.

Is the app really free?+

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no hidden "Pro" version.

Do I need an account or an Internet connection?+

No. There is no account and the app does not even have permission to access the Internet: it works anywhere, airplane mode included.

Is my data sent anywhere?+

Never. Collections, history and themes stay on your phone. Details are in the privacy policy.

The timer stops when I turn off the screen. What can I do?+

It should keep going thanks to an ongoing notification. Check that notifications are allowed for Pomodoro Chat and, on some phones (Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung…), turn off battery optimisation for the app.

Is it suitable for middle and high schoolers?+

That is exactly who it was made for: a simple interface, a funny mascot, no ads and no data collection. Perfect for homework, revision or practising an instrument.

Can I use my own images and sounds?+

Yes: create a custom theme, then replace the resting cat, the active cat, the paw or the background with your own images. Start, period-change and end sounds can be customised too.

Is there an iPhone version?+

Not yet: Pomodoro Chat is available on Android.

Privacy policy▾

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Pomodoro Chat is a focus timer published by Ronron Studio. In short: the app collects no personal data, shows no ads, requires no account and does not even have permission to access the Internet. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Data we collect

None. Ronron Studio does not receive, store or share any data about you. The app does not request the Android "Internet" permission: it is technically unable to send anything to a server.

There are no ads, no analytics, no automatic crash reporting and no embedded third-party services.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following locally, in its private storage: your collections and their periods (names, durations, Activity/Rest type), your session history (start and end times, completed or interrupted), your settings (theme, language, focus mode) and your custom themes.

If you pick a personal image or sound for a theme, a copy of the selected file is placed in the app’s private storage. Only the file you choose through the system picker is read; the app has no access to the rest of your photos or files.

This data never leaves your device because of Pomodoro Chat.

3. Permissions

Notifications: show the running timer and tell you when a period ends, even with the screen off. You can decline; the timer still works while the app is open.

Foreground service: keeps Android from stopping the timer when the screen is off or you switch apps.

No other permission is requested (no location, contacts, microphone, camera or Internet).

4. Android backup

If you turned on device backup (Google One / Android backup), the system may include the app’s data in the encrypted backup of your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google according to your settings; Ronron Studio has no access to it. You can turn it off in Android settings.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete a collection, a history entry or a theme directly in the app. To erase everything: Android Settings > Apps > Pomodoro Chat > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app.

6. Children and teens

Pomodoro Chat is designed for teenagers and suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected, no information about a minor is ever sent to Ronron Studio or any third party, and the app contains neither ads nor in-app purchases.

7. Windows version

The desktop version works the same way: all data stays in the computer’s user folder, with no network traffic at all.

8. Changes

If the way the app works changes (for example an optional sync feature), this policy will be updated on this page, with a new date, before that version is released.
